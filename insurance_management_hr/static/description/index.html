Gestión de seguros - Empleados

Employees as insured persons

Bridge between Gestión de seguros and Employees: installs automatically.

Key Features

  • Employee link: insured objects of person branches (personal accident, life) point to the employee.
  • Insurance history: the employee shows every policy they were insured in and their claims.
  • Claims: choosing the employee on a claim proposes the policy covering them.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
