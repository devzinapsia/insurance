===========================
Gestión de seguros - Ventas
===========================

Bridge between **Gestión de seguros** (``insurance_management``) and
**Sales** (``sale``). It installs automatically when both modules are
present.

* Surety bonds get a *Guaranteed sale order* (tab *Surety bond*), copied to
  their renewals, visible to sales users.
* The sale order shows the *Surety bonds* smart button (the last policy of
  each renewal chain).

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Nothing to configure.

Usage
=====

#. In the *Surety bond* tab of the policy, choose the guaranteed sale order.
#. From the sale order, open *Surety bonds*.
