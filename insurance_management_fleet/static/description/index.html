Gestión de seguros - Flota

Fleet vehicles as insured objects

Bridge between Gestión de seguros and Fleet: installs automatically.

Key Features

  • Vehicle link: insured objects of vehicle branches point to their fleet vehicle, linked automatically by license plate or chassis number (normalized, one match only).
  • Insurance history: the vehicle shows every policy it was insured in and its claims, and whether it is insured today.
  • Vehicles without insurance: filter in Fleet and figure in the insurance dashboard.
  • Required vehicle: branches can require a fleet vehicle on every insured object.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
