Gestión de seguros

Never let a company insurance policy expire unnoticed

Centralizes the insurance policies the company contracts: branches, insurers and producers, validity periods, renewals and expiry alerts, with documents and settings in one place.

Key Features

  • Two access levels: users manage policies; administrators also manage the configuration.
  • Insurers and producers as contacts: flags on the contact form with their own search filters.
  • Editable branches: ten initial branches with object type, alert days and users to notify, never overwritten by module updates.
  • Policies with a clear lifecycle: draft, in force, expiring, expired, renewed, not renewed and cancelled, with a reliable "covered today" indicator.
  • Validity checks: end after start, duration between configurable limits (warning or block), unique policy number per insurer, company and start date.
  • Insured objects with history: objects are removed with a date and a reason, never deleted, and every change is logged with old and new values.
  • Renewal and non-renewal: one click creates the next policy with its objects and coverages, linked to the previous one; "do not renew" emails the instruction to the producer and keeps it in the chatter.
  • Expiry alerts that never flood: thresholds such as 30, 15 and 7 days configurable by settings, branch and policy; one email per user in their language, an activity for the responsible, and a single notice when several thresholds are crossed at once.
  • Documents with a type: policy, certificate, receipts and more, uploaded from a Documents tab that also gathers the chatter attachments.
  • Hide costs: amounts protected on the server for users without the "see amounts" access.
  • Views for every need: kanban by state, calendar of expirations, pivot and graph, and an "upcoming expirations" report.
  • PDF reports: policy sheet and a list of the policies in force for audits or the producer.
  • Multi-company: record rules on every model, with branches that can be shared across companies.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
