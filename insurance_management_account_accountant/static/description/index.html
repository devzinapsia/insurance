Gestión de seguros - Diferidos de Enterprise

Accrue insurance premiums with the Enterprise deferred expenses

Bridge between Gestión de seguros and Odoo Enterprise accounting: installs automatically and adds the accrual method Enterprise deferred expenses.

Key Features

  • Deferral dates from the policy: bills created from a policy carry its validity (or the endorsement effective date) as deferral start and end dates.
  • Enterprise does the rest: the deferral entries are generated on validation or manually, with the deferred expense account of the accounting settings.
  • Running policies: installments billed after go-live are deferred from the go-live date.

Developed by Zinapsia for Odoo 19.
